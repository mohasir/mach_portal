Staff assignment is done **from the event**, not from the Staff section.

## How to assign

"Assign" button on the event page's Staff tab (or from the "..." menu in the table or the
calendar). The form asks for:

- **Available staff** (required) — a dropdown that only shows active staff.
- **Role** (optional, free text, e.g. "Bartender", "Server") — it's specific to that assignment,
  not a permanent attribute of the person.

![Form for assigning staff to an event](/support/asignar-personal-a-un-evento/1.png)

## How "available" works

Availability is calculated **by full day, not by time slot**. If someone is already assigned to
any other event that same day, they won't show up in the list — no matter if the events are at
different times (one in the morning and one at night both count as "busy" that day). If nobody's
available, the form shows "No staff available for this date".

## Restrictions

- You can't assign the same person twice to the same event.
- You can't assign staff to an event that's already **Completed** — the button simply isn't there.

## Removing someone

"Remove" button next to each person on the Staff tab, with confirmation. Unlike assigning,
**removing works no matter the event's status** (even if it's already Completed).
