A client's page (`Clients → [client]`) shows their info and two history tabs.

## Quotes and Events

- **Quotes**: a table with every quote for that client + a "New quote" button that takes you
  straight to the builder with the client already preselected.
- **Events**: a table with every event for that client.

![Quotes and Events tabs on a client's page](/support/historial-y-estado-del-cliente/1.png)

## Lead or Active

The client's status (the badge in the list and the page) **isn't picked by hand** — it's
calculated automatically:

- **Active**: if the client has at least one quote in the Confirmed stage.
- **Lead**: in any other case.

There's no way to force the status to "Active" without that confirmed quote existing.

![The Lead/Active status badge](/support/historial-y-estado-del-cliente/2.png)
