User account management (`Users`) is handled by Mach Bar's support team — it doesn't show up in
the menu for any client role.

## Requesting a new user

Write to the support team with the new person's name, email and the role they need. See
[The system's roles explained](/admin/settings/support/roles-y-permisos-explicados) to pick the
right one before you ask for it.

## Changing someone's role

This is also requested from support, telling them the new role.

## Removing an account

Ask support to delete the account. It's a real deletion: deleting it cascades to their sessions,
nothing is left dangling.
