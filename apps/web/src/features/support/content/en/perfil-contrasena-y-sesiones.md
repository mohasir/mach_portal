This section is about your own account — any user can see it, no matter their role.

## Profile

`Settings → Profile`: you can edit your Name. Email and role are shown but aren't editable from
here — if you need those changed, ask Mach Bar's support team.

Right there you'll also find the "Change password" card: it asks for your current password, the
new one (minimum 8 characters) and its confirmation.

![Profile screen with the change password card](/support/perfil-contrasena-y-sesiones/1.png)

## Active sessions

`Settings → Security`: lists the devices/browsers with a session logged in under your account
(with IP and last activity), marks which one is the current session, and lets you **revoke** the
others (you can't revoke the one you're currently using).

Use this if you lost a device or logged in on a computer that isn't yours and forgot to log out.

![List of active sessions](/support/perfil-contrasena-y-sesiones/2.png)
