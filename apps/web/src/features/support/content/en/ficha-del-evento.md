The event's page (`/admin/events/[id]`) has a fixed header and several tabs.

![Event page tabs](/support/ficha-del-evento/1.png)

## Header

Always visible: quote number, status tag, event type, date, time and address. A "View quote"
button (always available, takes you to the originating quote). If the event is Upcoming, you'll
also see "Mark as completed" and "Cancel event".

## Services included

The stations and their options, as they ended up in the quote. If you have permission to manage
selections, each line has buttons to view/edit its selection; otherwise it's read-only.

## Staff

List of assigned staff + an "Assign" button (visible while the event isn't Completed). See
[Assigning staff to an event](/admin/settings/support/asignar-personal-a-un-evento) for the full
detail.

## Payments and Attachments

A summary of paid/balance/total, the registration form and the payment history; attachments
(receipts) are grouped separately. **These two tabs don't exist at all if you don't have payment
permission** — they don't show up disabled, they simply aren't shown.

## What can't be edited from here

Client, date/time, address, event type and total amount aren't touched from the event's page —
that lives in the originating quote (see
[How an event is created and what it inherits from the quote](/admin/settings/support/como-se-crea-un-evento)).
