Just like with clients, a staff member can be **deleted for real** — but if they have event
assignments, the deletion will fail with a generic server error, not a clear message like "this
person has assigned events".

## What to do instead

If someone stops working but has a history of assigned events, it's better to **deactivate** them
(the Active/Inactive switch on their page) instead of trying to delete them:

- They stop showing up as available for new event assignments.
- It doesn't break anything they already had assigned on past events.
- They can be reactivated at any time if they come back to work.

Save "Delete" for people who were added by mistake and don't have any assignment yet.
