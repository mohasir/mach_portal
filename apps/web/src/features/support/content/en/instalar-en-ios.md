Mach Portal can be added to an iPhone or iPad's home screen to open like an app, without Safari's
address bar. It's not an App Store download — it's a shortcut that uses the same browser.

![Safari's Share menu with the "Add to Home Screen" option](/support/instalar-en-ios/1.png)

## How to install it

This has to be done from **Safari** — other browsers on iOS (Chrome, Firefox) don't offer this
option, even if they're installed on the device.

**Step 1:** Open Mach Portal in Safari and sign in normally.

**Step 2:** Tap the Share icon (the square with an arrow pointing up), in the bottom bar.

**Step 3:** Scroll down in the menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.

**Step 4:** Confirm the name ("Mach Portal" shows up by default) and tap **"Add"**, top right.

The icon shows up on the home screen alongside other apps. Opening it takes up the whole screen,
without Safari's address bar.
