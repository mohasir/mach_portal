`Settings → Preferences` is managed by Mach Bar's support team — it doesn't even show up in your
Settings menu. These are the two things you can ask to be changed there.

## Sortable catalog

A switch that turns drag & drop on/off for Stations, Services and Items in the catalog. If it's
off, the drag icon disappears everywhere in the app and the order stays fixed as saved.

## Choosing options when quoting

A switch that enables (or not) the "Choose options now" toggle inside the quote builder:

- **On**: staff sees the switch in the builder and can choose to resolve each station's options
  right when quoting.
- **Off**: the switch doesn't show up, and **every** new quote defers option selection until the
  event.

Quotes that already existed keep whatever they had saved even if this value changes afterward.
See
[Choosing options when quoting or leaving them for the event](/admin/settings/support/elegir-opciones-al-cotizar)
for the full detail.
