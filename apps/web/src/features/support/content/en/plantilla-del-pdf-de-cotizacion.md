`Settings → PDF template` (Admin role) controls the fixed text printed on any quote's PDF.

![PDF template](/support/plantilla-del-pdf-de-cotizacion/1.png)

## What's configurable

- **Service duration**: "Station: Duration" pairs (e.g. "Snack Station" / "Up to 2 hours of
  service") printed in the PDF's duration section.
- **Terms and conditions**: text where each line is a term — they show up as bullet points on the
  PDF. **If you don't enter any, that section simply doesn't appear** on the PDF.
- **Validity note**: free text next to the totals (e.g. "prices are valid for...").
- **Dietary note**: shows up at the bottom of "Services included" on the PDF, only if the quote
  has items with details entered.

## Important

These changes **don't affect PDFs already generated** — they'll only show up on the next PDFs
generated (or regenerated) after you save the template.
