Staff is a simple contact record — it doesn't have its own role or specialty field.

## Adding someone

From `Staff → New`: Name (required), Email and Phone (optional), and the Active/Inactive switch.

![The "New" form under Staff](/support/alta-y-asignacion-de-personal/1.png)

## The "role" belongs to the event, not the person

There's no permanent role field on a staff member's page. The "role" (e.g. "Bartender", "Server")
only shows up when you **assign** someone to a specific event — it's free text per assignment,
meant to note their function on that particular event, not a fixed attribute of the person.

## Where you see which events they're on

There's no "assigned events" tab on the Staff page — assignment is always managed **from the
event**, not from here. See
[Assigning staff to an event](/admin/settings/support/asignar-personal-a-un-evento) for the full
detail (including the full-day availability rule).
