`Settings → General → Quote stages` is managed by Mach Bar's support team — it doesn't show up in
your Settings, even though it shares a screen with other cards you can edit.

## What you can ask support to change

The 4 pipeline stages (Pendiente, Enviada, Aprobada, Cancelada) are **fixed** — they can't be
created or deleted. The only thing editable per stage is:

- **Name**: the text shown throughout the app (pipeline, tags, filters).
- **Color**: any hex value.
- **Description**: optional, informational text.

## One thing to keep in mind

Stage names **aren't translated** — whatever text gets entered is what shows up no matter the
interface language (Spanish or English). If your team works in both languages, mention that to
support when requesting the names.

Changing a stage's name or color applies immediately to every quote currently in that stage — you
don't need to touch quotes one by one.
